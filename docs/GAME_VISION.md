# GAME VISION

APHELION is a single-player generational strategy game about carrying civilization, not winning battles.

Six Arkships leave a ruined Earth with ~2.7M people toward Eiren IV, ~52 light-years away, ~550 years sailing. Nobody departing arrives. The player is Fleet Command continuity: keeping people alive, fed, healthy, and coherent long enough that descendants might arrive as something still recognizably human.

Four experiences interlock:

1. **Interstellar strategy** — routes, exploration, threats, resources; when to fight, hide, negotiate, or lose twenty years diverting.
2. **Arkship management** — aggregate populations (never millions of nodes), districts, emergencies, research, inter-ship relations. Every number is people.
3. **Real-time combat** — Space-Invaders-descended interceptor defence. Behind the player is a hull full of civilians; missed shots and breaches kill named-scale populations and persist.
4. **Generational history** — characters age and die; decisions become holidays, grievances, myths. Year-31 rationing can cause Year-186 rebellion.

Design rules: statistics are lives; combat ↔ civilization both ways; history persists; societies drift; aliens have motives, not just colors; simulation serves decisions; scale is shown, not just numbered; systems before content; data-driven events; always runnable.

The vertical slice proves the loop with one ship, one region, five events, one combat, and full save/continue before any of the ten future milestones (fleet, generations, society, exploration, contact, war, fracture, transformation, long migration).
