# STORY BIBLE (CANON FOR THE SLICE)

## The End of Earth
Late 22nd century: overlapping climate, resource, orbital, biological, and automated-warfare collapses. Earth survives; global civilization does not. Six Interstellar Continuity Arks are the only escape. No rescue follows.

## The Arkships (departure roles — not destinies)
- **Asteria** (playable): fleet command, administration, navigation. ~450,000 souls.
- **Demeter**: agriculture, ecology, biological reserves.
- **Daedalus**: manufacturing, engineering, heavy industry.
- **Sagan**: science, computation, research.
- **Mosaic**: culture, education, genetic repositories, civilian institutions.
- **Vanguard**: defence, reconnaissance, fleet security.

Total: ~2.7M. Roles may invert over centuries.

## The Destination
Eiren system (~50 ly), Eiren IV: water, atmosphere, possible biosphere from telescopes. No probe has been. Arrival is hope, not promise — inhabited, hostile, claimed, or eclipsed by en-route opportunities are all possible.

## Generation Zero
Departing generation remembers oceans, rain, stadiums, mosques/churches/temples. Children learn Earth geography they will never walk. The last Earth-memory dies within a lifetime; identity shifts from "from Earth" to "from Demeter."

## Life inside
Schools, love, crime, strikes, discoveries, lies, art, schisms, rationing, privacy vs. recycling. Fleet Command allocates electricity between hospitals and weapons, food after failed harvests, aid between ships.

## First Darkness (beyond the slice, foreshadowed)
Strange signals, lost probes, impossible maneuvers, derelicts — then living contacts: traders, trespass-claimants, nomads, machine remnants, swarmers. Some will try to destroy the fleet. Combat is campaigns and quiet decades, not daily apocalypse.

## Internal enemy
Asteria navigation authority, Vanguard protection terms, Demeter food sharing, Eiren's legitimacy — separatism, smuggling, politicized officers, sacred-journey vs. settle-now vs. nomad-forever vs. redesign-humanity theologies.

## Generations & history
Lifespans stay human. Commanders die mid-research; Year-47 heroes become Year-147 slogans become Year-347 disputed myths. The fleet log records rationing, refusals, destroyed settlements, rescues — with morale/relationship effects.

## Transformation question
Fusion, railguns, drones, closed ecologies, genetic medicine at departure. Centuries add cybernetics, germline divergence, machine integration or refusal, alien materials. Ships stop resembling departure hulls. Central question (asked, never dictated): after 500 years of change, what must stay unchanged to still be humanity?

## Opening (canonical text)
